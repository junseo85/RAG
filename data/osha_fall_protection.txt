1926.501 Duty to have fall protection

(a) General. Employers must determine if the walking/working surfaces on which
employees will work have the strength and structural integrity to support them safely.

(b)(1) Unprotected sides and edges. Each employee on a walking/working surface with an
unprotected side or edge which is 6 feet (1.8 m) or more above a lower level shall be
protected from falling by guardrail systems, safety net systems, or personal fall
arrest systems.

(b)(13) Residential construction. Employees engaged in residential construction
activities 6 feet or more above lower levels shall be protected by guardrail systems,
safety net system, or personal fall arrest system.

1926.502 Fall protection systems criteria and practices

(b) Guardrail systems. Top edge height of top rails shall be 42 inches (1.1 m), plus or
minus 3 inches. Midrails shall be installed halfway between the top edge and the
walking/working level. Guardrail systems shall be capable of withstanding, without
failure, a force of at least 200 pounds applied within 2 inches of the top edge.

(d) Personal fall arrest systems. Lanyards and vertical lifelines shall have a minimum
breaking strength of 5,000 pounds. Systems shall limit maximum arresting force on an
employee to 1,800 pounds when used with a body harness.
