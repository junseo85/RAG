# Municipal Public Works: Trench and Excavation Procedure  (rev. 4, scanned/OCR)

Pg. 1

## 1. Scope
This procedure applies to all excavations in the public right-of-way performed by city
crews or contractors.  Call 811 for utility locates at least 2 working days before digging.

## 2. Protective systems
Excavations 5 feet (1.5 m) or deeper require a protective system (sloping, shielding,
or shoring) unless excavated entirely in stable rock.  A competent person shall inspect
the excavation daily, before work starts, and after any rainstorm.

Excavations 20 feet deep or more require a protective system designed by a registered
professional engineer.

## 3. Access and egress
A stairway, ladder, or ramp must be located within 25 feet of lateral travel for
workers in trenches 4 feet or deeper.

## 4. Traffic control
Lane closures follow the MUTCD.  Flaggers must wear high-visibility Class 3 garments.
