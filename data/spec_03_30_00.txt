SECTION 03 30 00 - CAST-IN-PLACE CONCRETE
PART 1 - GENERAL
1.01 SUBMITTALS
A. Mix designs: submit for each class of concrete, with proof of 28-day strength.
1.02 QUALITY ASSURANCE
A. Testing: cast four cylinders per 50 cu. yd. or fraction thereof; test one at 7 days
   and two at 28 days.
PART 2 - PRODUCTS
2.01 CONCRETE MIX
A. Compressive strength: 4,000 psi at 28 days for slabs on grade.
B. Maximum water-cement ratio: 0.45.   Slump: 4 inches +/- 1 inch.
PART 3 - EXECUTION
3.01 CURING
A. Cure for a minimum of 7 days using wet curing or curing compound.
B. Do not place concrete when ambient temperature is below 40 deg F without cold weather protection.
